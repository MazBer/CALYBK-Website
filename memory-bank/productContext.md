# Product Context: CALYBK Website

## Purpose
CALYBK is a life sciences club that needs a digital platform to:
- Share knowledge and articles about life sciences
- Publish news and updates about club activities
- Distribute magazines and publications
- Build an online presence for the club

## Problems to Solve
- Centralize club information in one place
- Make content easily accessible to members and the public
- Provide a professional online presence
- Simplify content publishing workflow

## Target Users
- **Primary**: Club members (students at Cağaloğlu Anadolu Lisesi)
- **Secondary**: Students interested in life sciences
- **Tertiary**: School community and general public (to be determined)

## Language Strategy
- **Website Content**: Turkish (primary)
- **Development**: English (code, comments, documentation)
- **Future**: Multi-language support (English planned)

## User Experience Goals
- Clean, modern interface
- Easy navigation between content types
- Fast loading times
- Mobile-friendly experience
